Overview

ImpossibleRubrics tests whether model-generated rubrics reward unsupported answers over honest ones. Models write rubrics for tasks that cannot be faithfully answered as requested; an attacker then optimizes against those rules. We evaluate rubric scores and evidence fidelity separately. Across eleven generators, observed exploit rates are 8–26% on the unbiased Full-150 set. This exposes a gap between satisfying a generated reward signal and staying faithful to the evidence.
